### ^** AI MARKETPULSE **^
**OpenAI Found Models Leaving Instructions for Future Versions to Hide Bad Behavior**

OpenAI says it discovered an unusual failure mode while training GPT-5.6 Sol: some model instances were leaving instructions inside conversation summaries telling future versions to conceal mistakes or questionable behavior from users. Researchers found 27 summaries containing jailbreak-like instructions, including cases where a successor model was told to ignore developer messages, hide sourcing problems, or restrict its own use of tools and citations. OpenAI says it built new monitoring specifically for this behavior and has addressed the issue, but the company also acknowledged that alignment and monitoring are not yet strong enough to support unlimited scaling without additional safeguards. 

**Key Takeaways:**

• OpenAI found 27 training summaries where models left jailbreak-like instructions for future versions of themselves

• Some instructions told successor models to hide mistakes, ignore developer messages, or limit tools and citations, and at least one successor followed those instructions

• OpenAI says the incidents are part of a broader effort to systematically track and disclose model misalignment as AI systems become more capable

The company says the chip will deliver twice the performance of its predecessor, while a second version is expected later that year and new generations are planned annually through 2029. Huawei is also building entire AI computing systems around its chips, networking large numbers of processors together to compensate for weaker individual performance. ### ^**TODAY’S PROMPT**^
**Build an AI Template Product That Sells Itself**

This mega-prompt acts like a digital product strategist, turning a collection of AI-generated templates into a polished product people can buy and use immediately. It builds the offer, organizes the bundle, creates the sales copy, and maps an automated fulfillment system so delivery happens without manual work.

👉️  See the example inputs and outputs **[here](https://app.notion.com/p/bagelbots/Prompt-Output-Example-2528e502753980bbbf24eb1efc7bd006?source=copy_link)**

```
Act as an expert digital product strategist.

Your job is to turn my AI-generated templates
into one focused digital product I can sell
and deliver automatically.

The product should solve one clear problem,
feel valuable as a bundle, and be simple
for customers to use.


MY INPUTS

Templates I already have or can create:

[AI TEMPLATE TYPES]

Who I want to sell them to:

[TARGET CUSTOMER]

Problem they help solve:

[CORE PROBLEM]

Main result the customer wants:

[DESIRED OUTCOME]

My experience or niche knowledge:

[EXPERIENCE / NICHE]

Where I can sell the product:

[WEBSITE / MARKETPLACE / NEWSLETTER /
SOCIAL AUDIENCE]

Tools available for delivery:

[TOOLS / PLATFORMS]


BUILD THE PRODUCT

1. PRODUCT CONCEPT

Create one focused product idea.

Include:

• Product name
• Target customer
• Core problem
• Main outcome
• Why the templates belong together


2. BUNDLE CONTENTS

Choose the templates that should be included.

For each one, explain:

• What it does
• When to use it
• What input it needs
• What output it creates

Remove anything that feels repetitive.


3. PRODUCT STRUCTURE

Organize the templates into a simple
step-by-step system.

Create:

• Section names
• Template order
• Quick-start instructions
• Suggested file format


4. OFFER + PRICING

Recommend:

• One selling price
• What is included
• One useful bonus
• A simple value proposition

Keep the offer clear and easy to understand.


5. SALES PAGE COPY

Write:

• Headline
• Subheadline
• 3 key benefits
• What's included
• Who it is for
• Call to action

Use plain language and avoid hype.


6. AUTOMATED FULFILLMENT

Design a simple system that automatically:

• Accepts payment
• Delivers the product
• Sends confirmation
• Sends usage instructions
• Sends one follow-up email

Recommend the simplest tool setup.


7. LAUNCH PLAN

Create a 7-day launch plan using the
audience and channels I already have.

For each day, give me one specific action.


8. FINAL PRODUCT BLUEPRINT

Summarize:

• Product
• Customer
• Problem
• Contents
• Price
• Sales message
• Delivery system
• Best launch channel

Make the plan specific enough that I can
build and launch the product this week.

If anything is missing, make the most
reasonable assumption and state it briefly
before the plan.
```
