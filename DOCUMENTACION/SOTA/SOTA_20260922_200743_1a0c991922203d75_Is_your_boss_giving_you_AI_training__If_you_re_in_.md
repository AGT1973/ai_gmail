Is your employer giving you AI training?
****************************************

 

If you're in Europe, probably not. McKinsey's 2026 HR Monitor puts the share of European employees who received formal GenAI training at 25%. In the US it's 31%. In China, 49%.

That's not for lack of appetite. Deloitte surveyed 25,000 UK workers and found they spend an estimated £958 million a year of their own money on gen AI tools for work. Nearly a third use AI without telling their employer.

Despite geographical differences in the formal AI training offered, our own customer data shows no major difference in appetite for it.

Given the opportunity, European professionals take up AI training as readily as their peers across the Atlantic. Among companies that already invest in training, the share of AI courses started in the last 12 months was 44.3% in North America and 42% in Europe.

UK (39.3%) tracks behind the European average, while our customers in France (49.5%) and Spain (51.4%) are slightly ahead of the US average in course starts.

The lack of training doesn't stop professionals from applying AI at work. Deloitte also found that around half of employees who use AI at work have had no formal training in how to use it safely or effectively.

If you're one of the people funding this yourself, you've already decided it matters. And the longer you wait for a training budget that may or may not arrive, the more it costs you long-term.
